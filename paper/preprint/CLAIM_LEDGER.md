## Language that is safe to reuse

> We evaluate latent communication with a receiver-native oracle anchor, equal-capacity identity shuffles, and sealed task-level functional confirmation. In the tested Llama carrier, native residual packets transmitted task identity; a registered DeepSeek-to-Llama learned bridge did not, despite successful development geometry.

## Language to avoid

- “the first universal latent language”;
- “semantic translation between arbitrary LLMs”;
- “the learned bridge works”;
- “the boundary is irrelevant”;
- “the packet is minimal”;
- “Google Scholar/DOI guarantees scientific priority.”

## Evidence rule

`claim_eligible=true` means the registered protocol and evaluator were eligible to support a claim. It does not turn a failed hypothesis into a positive result. The semantic outcome is read from the registered contrasts and `semantic_transport_supported`.
